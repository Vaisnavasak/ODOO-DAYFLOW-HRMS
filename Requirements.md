👨‍💼 HR



HR manages employees.



👨‍💻 Employee



Employee manages/views their own information.





1.Login

Employee/HR logs in.

2\. Employees

HR can see and manage employees.

3\. Profile

Employee can see their personal/job/salary information.

4\. Attendance

Employee clicks Check In / Check Out.

HR can see everyone's attendance.

5\. Leave

Employee asks for leave.

HR approves or rejects it.

6\. Payroll

Employee sees their salary.

HR manages salary information.

7\. Database

Stores all this information.
