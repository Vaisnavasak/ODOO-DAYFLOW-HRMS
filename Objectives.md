\###🎯 Objectives of Dayflow

1\. Digitize HR activities and reduce manual paperwork.

2\. Maintain employee information such as personal, job, salary, and document details in one place.

3\. Provide secure login for employees and HR/Admin users.

4\. Manage employee attendance digitally through check-in, check-out, and attendance records.

5\. Allow employees to apply for leave digitally by selecting the leave type and dates.

6\. Enable HR to approve or reject leave requests and maintain the approval records.

7\. Provide salary and payroll information to employees and HR/Admin.

8\. Provide role-based access, so employees and HR can access only the functions relevant to them.

9\. Make HR information easy to access and manage through dashboards and employee records.

10\. Improve the efficiency and accuracy of HR operations by replacing traditional manual processes with a centralized digital system.
